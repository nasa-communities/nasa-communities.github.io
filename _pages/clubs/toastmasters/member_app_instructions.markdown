## Club 3496 • District 220

# Membership Application Instructions

*Last updated: August 2026*

## Overview {#overview}

Welcome to Goddard Toastmasters!

We're excited that you're interested in joining our club. Becoming a member is straightforward, and our club officers will guide you through each step of the process.

### Quick Facts

- **Club Number:** 3496
- **Club Name:** Goddard Toastmasters
- **District:** 220
- **Meeting Schedule:** Second and fourth Wednesdays, 11:30 a.m. – 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time
- **Meeting Format:** Virtual (see the [Club Home Page](/clubs/toastmasters/) for current meeting information)

### Who Can Join?

Goddard Toastmasters welcomes NASA Goddard civil servants, contractors, interns, and other members of the Goddard community who want to improve their communication and leadership skills.

Guests are always welcome to visit before deciding to join.

---

## How to Join {#how-to-join}

### Step 1 — Visit a Meeting

Attend one or more club meetings as a guest to experience our supportive learning environment.

### Step 2 — Talk with the VP Membership

If you decide to join, contact our Vice President Membership (or any club officer). They will guide you through the enrollment process and answer any questions.

### Step 3 — Complete the Membership Application

A club officer will provide the appropriate Toastmasters International membership application or online enrollment instructions.

Please have the following information available:

- Full name
- Preferred email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address

### Step 4 — Submit Payment

Membership fees are described below. The VP Membership or Treasurer will calculate the exact amount due based on when you join.

Payment instructions will be provided during the enrollment process.

## Membership Fees {#membership-fees}

### Toastmasters International Fees

- **New Member Fee:** **$25.00** (one-time fee for new members)
- **Membership Dues:** **$72.00** every six months
  - Toastmasters International dues are prorated depending on when you join.

### Goddard Toastmasters Club Dues

- **Club Dues:** **$6.00 per year**

### Civil Servants

NASA Goddard civil servants generally pay only the annual **$6.00 club dues**. Toastmasters International membership dues are typically processed through the GSFC Education Office as a professional development expense.

Please verify current procedures with the VP Membership.

### Contractors

Contractors are responsible for paying:

- Toastmasters International dues
- New Member Fee (new members only)
- Annual club dues

Some employers may reimburse professional development expenses. Check with your employer's HR or training office for details.

### Renewing Members

Membership renewals occur twice each year:

- **April 1 – September 30**
- **October 1 – March 31**

Current renewal costs:

- **Civil Servants:** $6.00 annual club dues
- **Contractors:** $144.00 Toastmasters International dues per year + $6.00 annual club dues

## After You Join {#after-you-join}

Once your membership has been processed, you can expect the following:

### Toastmasters Membership

- Your membership will be activated by Toastmasters International.
- You'll receive access to your Toastmasters profile and educational resources.

### Education Program

You'll gain access to the Toastmasters Education Program (Pathways), where you'll:

- Choose a learning path
- Complete communication and leadership projects
- Begin preparing your first speech

### Club Participation

You'll also:

- Be added to the club email distribution list
- Receive meeting agendas and announcements
- Be assigned a mentor (when available)
- Begin participating in meeting roles, Table Topics®, and prepared speeches

Remember—there is no pressure to speak immediately. Participate at your own pace while becoming familiar with the meeting format.
