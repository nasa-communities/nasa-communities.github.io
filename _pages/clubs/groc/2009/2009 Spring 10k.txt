"10-Km Race at Greenbelt Lake, 4/22/2009"										

A cool rain kept some away but the ones who came ran
pretty well.  The rain stopped and the temperature was
good for a 10k (low 50s).  Mark Wojenski ran the Boston
Marathon two days before but easily shook off any
stiffness after the 1st lap and won in 38:20.  Care
Salustro made her NASA 10k debut and was 1st female.
